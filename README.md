# TECH-V Air Conditioning Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

TECH-V Cool Workz Pte. Ltd. provides aircon servicing, maintenance, repairs, and installation for homes and businesses in Singapore. The redesigned homepage helps visitors compare residential maintenance plans, understand what servicing covers, and request a quotation.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make maintenance plans easier to compare.** The draft shows three-, four-, and six-visit plans side by side and aligns published prices by fan-coil count. It describes the routine servicing scope and states that the published residential prices include GST. Whether contract prices are annual totals or per-service charges still needs confirmation.
2. **Explain service scope and pricing conditions.** The draft gives general servicing, chemical wash, repairs, and installation distinct service cards. It places system exclusions, the S$10 surcharge for CBD, Sentosa Island, and Jurong Island addresses, and the separate quotation requirement for fan coils above 24,000 BTU beside the residential price tables.
3. **Make enquiries easier and give customer feedback a dedicated section.** The draft highlights free on-site quotations and includes a form that opens WhatsApp with the selected service, fan-coil count, and property type. A fixed mobile bar keeps Call and WhatsApp accessible. Review cards need genuine customer excerpts, and project photography, operating hours, and the customer-facing address still need confirmation.

## Design Improvements

Consistent plan cards and aligned price tables make options easier to scan. A numbered servicing checklist and booking walkthrough show what a visit covers and what customers should share when requesting a quote.
