Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should I service my aircon?

It depends on usage, environment and system condition. Tech-V lists maintenance options with three, four and six visits per year. Ask the team which frequency suits your usage.

What is included in standard servicing?

The published scope includes filter and blower checks, cooling coil cleaning, drainage flushing and checks on selected components. Chemical flushing is excluded from the standard scope.

Are the published prices for all aircon types?

No. The residential pricing excludes ducted, ceiling cassette and VRV systems. Fan coils above 24,000 BTU require a separate quotation.

Are there location surcharges?

The pricing page states a S$10 surcharge per service for CBD, Sentosa Island and Jurong Island addresses. Confirm the current charge when booking.

Can I request an installation quotation?

Yes. The website states that free on-site quotations are available. Share your property and system requirements to arrange an assessment.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp Tech-V with your unit count, property type, service requirement and preferred appointment timing.
